A few minutes to set up.
Then one click to save what matters.

  1. Install the extension

    Download and extract the ZIP from your Gumroad purchase. In desktop Chrome, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and use Load unpacked to select the folder containing manifest.json.

  2. Make it yours

    In the extension’s Settings, choose your Obsidian vault folder and allow write access. Paste your personal license key to activate Lifetime Pro. Use version 1.0.2 or later.

  3. Save your next idea

    Open a Gemini conversation, load the content you want to keep, and click Export to Obsidian. Your conversation becomes a Markdown note in your selected folder.

What is included in my purchase?

Your Gumroad purchase includes the extension ZIP, installation instructions and a personal license key. Lifetime Pro unlocks full-length exports for a one-time payment of US$9.99. There is no subscription. Applicable taxes and the final total are shown at checkout.

How is Pro different from the free plan?

The free plan exports the first 500 characters of the conversation body. Pro removes that limit, so you can save the full conversation content loaded on the page, including loaded Deep Research reports.

Can I use it on my phone?

The extension is designed for desktop Chrome or a compatible Chromium browser with File System Access support. It requires permission to write to your selected vault folder. This website works on mobile, but the extension does not run in mobile Chrome, Safari or Firefox.

How do I include a Deep Research report?

Open the report panel in Gemini and scroll through it to load the report before exporting. Only content currently loaded on the page can be exported. Long conversations may also need to be scrolled through first.

Where does my conversation data go?

The extension processes conversation content locally and writes Markdown to the folder you select. License verification contacts Gumroad; your conversations are not sent to Gumroad. See the Privacy Notice for details.
