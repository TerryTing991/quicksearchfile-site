Privacy Notice

Gemini to Obsidian · Effective 5 June 2026

Summary

We collect as little data as possible. Your Gemini conversations are never sent to our servers — the Extension reads them in your browser and writes Markdown files directly into your own local Obsidian folder.

What we process

License key: when you activate or re-check a license, your key is sent to Lemon Squeezy's license API to verify its status. Lemon Squeezy stores the key, its plan, and activation records.

Local data: your settings (chosen folder, export preferences) and license status are stored locally in your browser only.

Payment data: handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store your card details. Lemon Squeezy shares your email and purchase information with us so we can issue and support your license.

Server logs

Lemon Squeezy may log standard request metadata (such as IP address and timestamp) when validating license keys, for security and reliability.

No data selling

We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties

Lemon Squeezy (payments, license management, and Merchant of Record) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each has its own privacy policy.
