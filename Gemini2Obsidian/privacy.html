Privacy Notice

Gemini to Obsidian · Updated 10 October 2026

Summary

We collect as little data as possible. Your Gemini conversations are never sent to our servers — the Extension reads them in your browser and writes Markdown files directly into your own local Obsidian folder.

Purchase availability and licensing transition

New purchases are temporarily unavailable while we prepare checkout through Gumroad and a compatible extension update. The existing extension version still uses Lemon Squeezy for license activation, daily validation, and deactivation. Updating this website does not change the licensing behavior of an installed extension.

What we process

Local data: your selected folder handle is stored in your browser's IndexedDB. Export preferences and cached license information are stored in browser extension storage. Your conversations are read from the Gemini page and saved locally; they are not uploaded to Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or our servers.

Existing extension license checks: if you enter a license key in an existing build, the key and, where applicable, its activation instance identifier are sent to Lemon Squeezy's license API to activate, validate, or deactivate that license. The key and license status are cached locally.

Future Gumroad purchases: when sales reopen, Gumroad will process checkout and provide us with purchase information such as your email address, product, order status, and license key for delivery, support, and refunds. Gumroad and its payment providers process the payment details; we do not receive or store your full card details. We will update this notice with the Gumroad license verification flow before releasing the compatible extension.

Support: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. Please avoid including private conversation content unless it is needed for your support request.

Request metadata

Website hosting, licensing, and payment providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses and timestamps for service operation, security, and fraud prevention. This product website does not include analytics scripts or an embedded checkout.

No data selling

We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties

Gumroad (planned checkout and purchase delivery), its payment providers (including Stripe and PayPal where applicable), Lemon Squeezy (license checks in existing extension builds), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Their respective privacy policies apply to data they process.

Your choices

You can remove the saved folder setting and license from the extension settings. Removing a license triggers a best-effort deactivation request in existing builds. Clearing extension data or uninstalling removes its locally stored data. Contact us about purchase or support data you want to access or delete; some transaction records may need to be retained for legal or accounting purposes.
