Support

Help with audio-file transcription, partial transcription, configurable summaries, exports, credits, and your data.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Contact Support

Email terryting991@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, 繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and the displayed error message. For transcription issues, include the audio format, duration, language, and processing stage. For credit issues, include the product and purchase date. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or confidential recordings.

Get Started

Choose an existing audio file in the app, or open a recording in Voice Memos and use Share → 繁字逐字稿. Supported audio includes m4a, mp3, wav, aac, and caf, with a maximum length of five hours. Review the cloud-processing consent and credit estimate, then start transcription with an internet connection. Open the completed record in transcript history to review the text and play back the processed recording. AI output can contain errors; check names, numbers, and important details against the original audio.

Improve Recognition

Use a clear recording with limited background noise and avoid overlapping speakers where possible. Before transcription, add names, organizations, units, and specialist terms to the custom vocabulary list. You can review and correct individual transcript lines after processing; later summaries and exports use those corrected lines.

Find a Previous Transcript

Open transcript history and use the calendar button to filter by date. Dates with records are available for selection; dates without records are gray. Choose “顯示全部” (Show All) to clear the filter. Records are grouped by their creation date in your device’s local calendar.

Create a Summary

Open a completed transcript and choose the summary action, or select a transcript from the Summary tab. Before creating it, choose at least one section: Key Summary, Previous Meeting Resolution Status, Reports, Discussions, Resolutions, Extempore Motions, Action Items, or Timeline. Your selection is remembered for the next summary. The number of selected sections does not change the price: up to 60 minutes costs 10 credits, over 60 and up to 180 minutes costs 15, and over 180 minutes costs 25. A failed summary is not charged or its charge is refunded.

Export Your Work

Open a transcript or summary and use its export menu, then choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Transcripts can be exported as plain text, Markdown, Word, PDF, or SRT subtitles. Summaries can be exported as plain text, Markdown, Word, or PDF. Exports are created on the device and include your transcript corrections.

How Transcription Credits Work

繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） uses consumable credits without a monthly subscription. Transcription costs 1 credit per whole minute of each audio file, with a minimum of 1 credit. For example, 10 minutes and 40 seconds costs 10 credits. Failed processing is not charged or the charge is refunded. Refer to the app for available packs and current purchase prices.

Transcribe Part of a Recording

If your balance cannot cover the full recording but is greater than zero, the app offers to transcribe only the first part. Use the stepper to choose the number of minutes in five-minute steps, up to the maximum your balance allows. When possible, the initial suggestion leaves enough credits to summarize that partial transcript. The app shows the remaining balance and whether it will cover a summary before you start. Only the selected beginning portion is processed, and the finished record identifies it as a partial transcript and shows the original recording length.

Introductory Credits

Eligible new users receive 60 credits after consent. This offer is limited to once per device and does not require a rating or review. Apple DeviceCheck helps prevent repeated claims. Completing a transcription or summary does not grant additional promotional credits.

A Job Is Taking Longer Than Expected

Check your internet connection and the status shown in the app. Longer recordings and service queues can take more time. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same file while a job is still processing. If a job remains stuck or displays an error, contact support with the processing stage, approximate start time, and audio duration.

A Purchase or Balance Looks Wrong

Check that Apple completed the purchase and reconnect to the internet so the app can verify the transaction. Credits belong to the app’s anonymous account; do not assume consumable purchases can be restored to a new account or transferred to another device. Avoid deleting your account to troubleshoot a balance. Contact support if the balance is still incorrect; Apple handles App Store refund requests.

Manage Consent and Delete Data

You can withdraw AI processing consent in Settings and continue viewing or exporting existing content. Long-press a transcript in history to delete it and its associated summary and recording. To remove the anonymous account, cloud content, local recordings, and remaining credits, choose “刪除帳號與所有資料” (Delete Account and All Data) in Settings. Wait for active jobs to finish first. Deletion is permanent; copies exported to other apps must be deleted separately.
