繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） for iOS

Support

Help with recording, transcription, summaries, exports, credits, and your data.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Contact Support

Email terryting991@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, 繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and the displayed error message. For transcription issues, include the audio format, duration, language, and processing stage. For credit issues, include the product and purchase date. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or confidential recordings.

Get Started

Allow microphone access to record, or import an audio file from Files. Review the cloud-processing consent and the credit estimate, then start transcription with an internet connection. Open the completed record in transcript history to review the text and play back the associated recording. AI output can contain errors; check names, numbers, and important details against the original audio.

Improve Recognition

Use a clear recording with limited background noise and avoid overlapping speakers where possible. Add names, organizations, and specialist terms to the custom vocabulary list before transcription. Check the selected language and review the generated transcript.

Find a Previous Transcript

Open transcript history and use the calendar button to filter by date. Dates with records are available for selection; dates without records are gray. Choose “顯示全部” (Show All) to clear the filter. Records are grouped by their creation date in your device’s local calendar.

Create a Summary

Open a completed transcript and choose the summary action, or select a transcript from the Summary tab. Review the displayed credit cost before confirming. Summary credits depend on audio length: up to 60 minutes costs 10 credits, over 60 and up to 180 minutes costs 15, and over 180 minutes costs 25. A failed summary is not charged or its charge is refunded.

Export Your Work

Open a transcript or summary and use its export menu, then choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Use the formats shown for that content, including Word or plain text where available. Save a copy to Files if you want to keep it outside the app.

How Transcription Credits Work

繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） uses consumable credits without a monthly subscription. Transcription costs 1 credit per whole minute of each audio file, with a minimum of 1 credit. For example, 10 minutes and 40 seconds costs 10 credits. Failed processing is not charged or the charge is refunded. Refer to the app for available packs and current purchase prices.

Introductory Credits

Eligible new users receive 60 credits after consent and another 60 after their first successful audio transcription. This offer is limited to once per device and does not require a rating or review. Apple DeviceCheck helps prevent repeated claims.

A Job Is Taking Longer Than Expected

Check your internet connection and the status shown in the app. Longer recordings and service queues can take more time. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same file while a job is still processing. If a job remains stuck or displays an error, contact support with the processing stage, approximate start time, and audio duration.

A Purchase or Balance Looks Wrong

Check that Apple completed the purchase and reconnect to the internet so the app can verify the transaction. Credits belong to the app’s anonymous account; do not assume consumable purchases can be restored to a new account or transferred to another device. Avoid deleting your account to troubleshoot a balance. Contact support if the balance is still incorrect; Apple handles App Store refund requests.

Manage Consent and Delete Data

You can withdraw AI processing consent in Settings and continue viewing or exporting existing content. Long-press a transcript in history to delete it and its associated summary and recording. To remove the anonymous account, cloud content, local recordings, and remaining credits, choose “刪除帳號與所有資料” (Delete Account and All Data) in Settings. Wait for active jobs to finish first. Deletion is permanent; copies exported to other apps must be deleted separately.
