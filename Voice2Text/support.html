開始使用

您可以在 App 中選擇既有錄音檔，或在「語音備忘錄」開啟錄音後，使用「分享」→「繁字逐字稿」。支援 m4a、mp3、wav、aac、caf 等音訊格式，單一錄音最長 5 小時。確認私密雲端資料處理同意與點數預估後，即可在有網路連線時開始高品質語音辨識與文字校對。完成後可在逐字稿歷史紀錄中檢查文字並播放對應錄音。AI 結果可能包含錯誤，重要姓名、數字與內容請與原始錄音核對。

提升逐字稿品質

逐字稿品質會受到原始錄音的清晰度、背景噪音、收音距離、說話音量及多人同時發言等因素影響。清楚且干擾較少的錄音通常能獲得較佳結果。開始前可將人名、組織、單位及專有名詞加入「常用詞彙」。處理後可逐句檢查與修正，後續摘要及匯出會使用修正後的內容。

查找過往逐字稿

開啟逐字稿歷史紀錄，使用月曆按鈕依日期篩選。有逐字稿的日期可供選擇，沒有逐字稿的日期會顯示為灰色。選擇「顯示全部」可清除日期篩選。紀錄會依裝置本地月曆中的建立日期分組。

產生摘要

開啟已完成的逐字稿並選擇摘要功能，或在「摘要」頁面選取逐字稿。產生前須至少勾選一個段落，可選重點摘要、前次會議決議辦理情形、報告事項、討論事項、決議事項、臨時動議、待辦事項或時間軸；選擇會保留供下次使用。段落數量不影響價格：錄音 1 小時內為 10 點、超過 1 小時至 3 小時為 15 點、超過 3 小時為 25 點。摘要處理失敗不扣點，或由系統自動退還。

匯出內容

開啟逐字稿或摘要並使用匯出選單，再於 iOS 分享選單中選擇目的位置。逐字稿可匯出為純文字、Markdown、Word、PDF 或 SRT 字幕；摘要可匯出為純文字、Markdown、Word 或 PDF。匯出檔案會在裝置上建立，並包含您對逐字稿所做的修正。

逐字稿點數計算

繁字逐字稿使用消耗型點數，沒有月租或訂閱。每 1 點可處理 1 分鐘錄音檔，單檔最低 1 點；超過 1 分鐘後只計完整分鐘，例如 10 分 40 秒扣 10 點。處理失敗不扣點，或由系統自動退還。可在 App 內查看目前提供的點數方案及實際售價。

製作部分逐字稿

若點數不足以處理完整錄音，但餘額大於 0，App 會提供只處理錄音開頭部分內容的選項。您可用步進控制以 5 分鐘為單位調整，最高不超過目前點數可負擔的長度。在點數允許時，預設選項會保留一次摘要所需的點數；開始前也會顯示完成後餘額及是否足以產生摘要。完成後會清楚標示這是部分逐字稿，並顯示原始錄音長度。

試用點數

符合資格的新用戶在同意資料處理後可獲得 60 點試用點數，每台裝置限領一次，且不需要評分或評論。Apple DeviceCheck 用於防止重複領取。完成逐字稿或摘要不會再額外贈送試用點數。

處理時間較長

請先確認網路連線及 App 顯示的處理狀態。較長的錄音與服務排隊可能需要更多時間；工作仍在處理時，請勿重複送出同一檔案。若工作長時間停留在同一階段或顯示錯誤，請聯絡支援並提供處理階段、約略開始時間及錄音長度。

購買或餘額異常

請確認 Apple 已完成交易，並重新連接網路，讓 App 驗證購買。點數屬於 App 建立的匿名帳號；消耗型購買不保證能還原至新帳號，也無法假定可移轉至其他裝置。請勿為排除餘額問題而刪除帳號。若餘額仍不正確，請聯絡支援；App Store 退款申請由 Apple 處理。

管理同意與刪除資料

您可在「設定」撤回 AI 資料處理同意，撤回後仍可查看或匯出既有內容。在逐字稿歷史紀錄中長按項目，可刪除該筆逐字稿、摘要與相關錄音。若要移除匿名帳號、私密雲端內容、本機錄音及剩餘點數，請在「設定」選擇「刪除帳號與所有資料」；請先等待進行中的工作完成。刪除後無法復原，已匯出至其他 App 的副本須另外刪除。

AccuScribe for iOS

Support

Help with English transcripts, summaries, exports, credits, and your data in AccuScribe.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Contact Support

Email terryting991@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, AccuScribe app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message shown. For transcription issues, include the audio format, duration, and processing stage. For credit issues, include the product and purchase date. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or confidential recordings.

Get Started

First, review the welcome screen’s disclosure of the data shared with OpenAI, Modal, and Google Firebase / Google Cloud. You can read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before tapping “I Agree and Start.” Then, on the New tab, tap Choose a Recording and pick an audio file from Files, or open a recording in Voice Memos and tap Share → AccuScribe. Supported formats are m4a, mp3, wav, aac, and caf, up to five hours long. Review the credit estimate, then tap Create Transcript. An internet connection is required. Open the finished item on the Transcripts tab to read the text and play back the recording. Transcripts can contain mistakes; check names, numbers, and important details against the original audio.

Improve Recognition

AccuScribe recognizes both American and British English and keeps each speaker's spelling. For the best results, use a clear recording with little background noise, keep the microphone close to the speakers, and avoid people talking over each other where possible. Before transcribing, add names, companies, products, and acronyms to Vocabulary so they are spelled correctly. After processing, you can touch and hold any line to correct it; summaries and exports use your corrected text.

Find a Previous Transcript

On the Transcripts tab, tap the calendar button to filter by date. Darker dates have transcripts; lighter dates have none. Tap Show All to clear the filter. Transcripts are grouped by the date they were created on your device.

Create a Summary

Open a finished transcript and tap the summary button, or select a transcript on the Summaries tab. Choose at least one section: Overview, Matters Arising, Reports, Discussion, Decisions, Other Business, Action Items, or Timeline. Your choice is remembered for next time. The number of sections doesn't change the price: recordings up to 1 hour cost 10 credits, 1 to 3 hours cost 15, and over 3 hours cost 25. If a summary fails, you are not charged, or the credits are refunded automatically. Tap any timeline point in a summary to jump back to that moment in the transcript.

Export Your Work

Open a transcript or summary, tap the export button, and choose a destination in the share sheet. Transcripts can be exported as plain text, Markdown, Word, PDF, or SRT subtitles. Summaries can be exported as plain text, Markdown, Word, or PDF. Exports are created on your device and include your corrections.

How Credits Work

AccuScribe uses consumable credits with no subscription. A transcript costs 1 credit per whole minute of each recording, with a minimum of 1 credit; for example, 10 minutes 40 seconds costs 10 credits. If processing fails, you are not charged, or the credits are refunded automatically. Current packs and prices are shown on the Credits tab.

Transcribe Part of a Recording

If your balance can't cover the whole recording but is above zero, the New tab offers to transcribe just the first part. Adjust the length in five-minute steps, up to what your balance allows. When possible, the suggested length leaves enough credits to summarize it. Only the selected beginning is processed, and the finished transcript notes that it covers part of the recording and shows the original length.

Free Credits for New Users

Eligible new users may receive 60 credits after accepting the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The offer is limited to once per device and does not require a rating or review. Apple DeviceCheck helps prevent repeated claims. If the Credits tab says your welcome credits require verification, contact support; your existing credits remain available.

A Job Is Taking Longer Than Expected

Check your internet connection and the status shown in the app. Longer recordings and busy periods can take more time. Please don't submit the same file again while it is still processing. If a job stays stuck or shows an error, contact support with the processing stage, approximate start time, and recording length.

A Purchase or Balance Looks Wrong

Make sure Apple completed the purchase, then reconnect to the internet so the app can verify it; credits that don't appear right away are added the next time you open the app. Credits belong to the app's anonymous account and cannot be restored to a new account or moved to another device, so please don't delete your account to troubleshoot a balance. Contact support if the balance is still wrong. Apple handles App Store refund requests.

Manage Consent and Delete Data

You can withdraw consent at the bottom of Settings › About › Privacy Policy and still view or export existing content. Touch and hold a transcript to delete it along with its summary and recording. To remove your anonymous account, cloud content, recordings on the device, and remaining credits, choose Delete Account and All Data in Settings. Wait for active jobs to finish first. Deletion is permanent; copies you exported to other apps must be deleted separately.
