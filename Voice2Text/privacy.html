隱私權政策

說明繁字逐字稿如何處理您選擇的錄音檔、逐字稿、摘要、匿名帳號與點數資料。繁字逐字稿（英文名稱 AccuScribe）提供繁體中文與英文介面，可在「設定 › 語言」切換。

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 14 日

蒐集與處理的資料

繁字逐字稿會處理您主動選擇或分享至 App 的錄音檔、逐字稿、逐字稿修正內容、摘要、所選摘要段落、常用詞彙、工作狀態、匿名帳號識別碼、點數餘額，以及 Apple App 內購買交易資料。為避免重複領取試用點數，App 也會使用 Apple DeviceCheck 的隱私保護裝置狀態，但不會保存原始 DeviceCheck token。App 不會蒐集聯絡人或位置資訊，也不會進行廣告追蹤。

處理目的

錄音檔與相關文字僅用於為您完成逐字稿、文字校對、摘要及匯出；其他帳號與交易資料僅用於登入、工作處理、點數計費、防止購買或新用戶優惠重複兌換及錯誤退款。資料不會被出售或出租，也不會用於行銷、建立廣告受眾、跨 App／網站追蹤，或任何與本 App 功能無關的目的。您的錄音檔、逐字稿與摘要不會被開發者或任何第三方服務用來訓練或改進模型。

私密雲端處理與您的同意

逐字稿與 AI 文字處理需要網路連線。開始製作逐字稿或摘要前，App 會先取得您對私密雲端及 AI 資料處理的同意。若點數不足而選擇製作部分逐字稿，App 會先在裝置上擷取錄音開頭的工作副本，只上傳您選擇的前段內容進行處理。您可在「設定」中撤回同意；撤回後仍可查看及匯出既有內容，但再次製作逐字稿或摘要前必須重新同意。撤回同意不會自動刪除既有內容，也不會取消已完成的處理。

第三方服務與傳送的資料

開始製作逐字稿或摘要前，App 會在同意畫面列出下列接收對象與傳送的資料，取得您的同意後才會傳送：

  • OpenAI（第三方 AI 服務）：接收逐字稿文字與您的常用詞彙，用於校對錯字、補上標點符號，以及產生您要求的摘要。透過 OpenAI API 傳送的資料不會用來訓練或改進 OpenAI 模型。
  • Modal（雲端運算服務）：接收錄音檔，用於執行 Breeze（中文錄音）與 Whisper（英文錄音）語音辨識模型，產生含時間戳記的逐字稿。錄音檔僅供語音辨識使用，不會用來訓練或改進任何模型。
  • Google Firebase／Google Cloud：保存錄音檔暫存、逐字稿、摘要、匿名帳號與點數紀錄，並提供匿名登入、資料庫與工作排程。資料僅供本 App 功能使用，不會用來訓練或改進任何模型。
  • Apple：處理 App 內購買，並透過 DeviceCheck 確認試用點數資格。

上述服務僅能為完成本 App 功能處理資料，並須依其服務條款提供與本政策相同或同等的資料保護。

本機儲存與匯出

匿名登入憑證會保存在 iOS Keychain。App 設定、摘要段落偏好、歷史紀錄、逐字稿修正內容，以及供播放使用的錄音檔可能保存在您的裝置上。逐字稿與摘要匯出檔案會在裝置上建立。當您匯出或分享內容時，所選的目的 App 或服務會處理該副本；在繁字逐字稿中刪除內容，不會刪除已匯出至其他位置的副本。

保存期間與刪除

上傳的錄音檔通常會在逐字稿完成後從私密雲端儲存空間刪除。此清理會盡力完成；處理失敗或中斷的上傳檔案可能保留至系統清理、您刪除單筆紀錄，或刪除帳號時。逐字稿、摘要及本機播放用錄音檔會保留至您刪除對應紀錄或帳號。

刪除單筆紀錄或帳號

在逐字稿歷史紀錄中長按項目，即可刪除該筆逐字稿、摘要與相關錄音檔，包括伺服器上的副本。若要刪除匿名帳號及相關內容，請開啟「設定」並選擇「刪除帳號與所有資料」。刪除前須等待進行中的逐字稿或摘要完成。刪除帳號也會移除剩餘點數與本機錄音檔，且無法復原。僅刪除 App 不等於要求刪除伺服器資料，Keychain 中的憑證也可能在重新安裝後繼續存在。

點數與購買紀錄

點數是由 Apple 處理的消耗型 App 內購買商品。繁字逐字稿不會取得您的付款卡資料。系統會保存點數餘額與交易紀錄，用於兌換購買、處理退款及防止重複兌換。刪除帳號後，系統仍會保留去識別化的交易編號、商品、金額及必要的會計或防詐資訊。App Store 購買紀錄與退款申請由 Apple 管理。

安全性

App 使用加密網路連線傳輸資料，並使用系統 Keychain 保存登入憑證。系統透過身分驗證與服務存取控制限制帳號內容的存取，但任何網路服務都無法保證絕對安全。

網站與支援信件

本靜態網站未加入廣告追蹤器或分析指令碼。網站託管服務可能為維運及安全目的處理 IP 位址、瀏覽器資訊與請求時間等基本資料。若您寄送支援信件，我們會收到您的電子郵件地址及您主動提供的資訊，以協助處理問題。除非確有必要，請勿寄送機密錄音或逐字稿。

政策更新

本政策可能因 App 功能、服務供應商或資料處理方式變更而更新。更新後的政策與日期會公布於本頁。

AccuScribe for iOS

Privacy Policy

How AccuScribe handles selected audio, partial transcription, transcript corrections, summaries, anonymous accounts, and credits.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Data We Process

AccuScribe processes audio you select or share with the app, transcripts, transcript corrections, summaries, selected summary sections, custom vocabulary, processing status, anonymous account identifiers, credit balances, and Apple in-app purchase transaction data. The app also uses Apple DeviceCheck device state to prevent repeated claims of introductory credits; it does not store raw DeviceCheck tokens. The app does not collect contacts or location information and does not perform advertising tracking.

Why We Process Data

Your audio and related text are used only to provide your transcripts, proofreading, summaries, and exports. Other account and transaction data is used only for sign-in, job processing, credit accounting, preventing duplicate redemption of purchases or new-user offers, and refunds after processing errors. Data is not sold or rented and is not used for marketing, advertising audiences, cross-app or cross-website tracking, or purposes unrelated to the app. Your audio, transcripts, and summaries are not used by the developer or any third-party service to train or improve models.

Private Cloud Processing and Your Consent

Transcription and AI text processing require an internet connection. Before transcription or summary generation, the app asks for your consent to private cloud and AI processing. If you choose a partial transcript because your balance cannot cover the full recording, the app trims a working copy on your device and uploads only the selected beginning portion for processing. You can withdraw consent in Settings. You can still view and export existing content after withdrawing consent, but must consent again before starting another transcription or summary. Withdrawing consent does not delete existing content or undo processing already performed.

Service Providers and Data Shared

Before any transcription or summary, the app's consent screen lists the recipients below and the data each receives. Nothing is sent until you agree.

  • OpenAI (third-party AI service): receives transcript text and your custom vocabulary to correct typos, add punctuation, and generate summaries you request. Data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train or improve OpenAI's models.
  • Modal (cloud computing service): receives audio to run the Whisper speech recognition model and produce a timestamped transcript. Audio is used only for speech recognition and is not used to train or improve any model.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: store temporary audio, transcripts, summaries, anonymous account and credit records, and provide anonymous sign-in, databases, and job scheduling. Data is used only for the app's features and is not used to train or improve any model.
  • Apple: handles in-app purchases and uses DeviceCheck to confirm introductory-credit eligibility.

These providers may process data only to provide the app's features and, under their terms, must protect it at a level equal to or greater than this policy.

Local Storage and Exports

Anonymous sign-in credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. App settings, summary-section preferences, history, transcript corrections, and recordings used for playback may be stored on your device. Transcript and summary exports are created on your device. When you export or share content, the destination app or service you choose handles that copy. Deleting content in AccuScribe does not delete copies you have exported elsewhere.

Retention and Deletion

Uploaded audio is normally removed from private cloud storage after transcription completes. This cleanup is best effort; failed or interrupted uploads may remain until cleanup, individual record deletion, or account deletion. Transcripts, summaries, and local playback recordings remain until you delete the corresponding record or account.

Delete a Record or Your Account

Long-press a record in transcript history to delete its transcript, summary, and associated recording, including the server copy. To delete the anonymous account and associated content, open Settings and choose “刪除帳號與所有資料” (Delete Account and All Data). Wait for active transcription or summary jobs to finish before deletion. Account deletion also removes remaining credits and local recordings and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app alone does not request deletion of server data; Keychain credentials may also survive reinstallation.

Credits and Purchase Records

Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. AccuScribe does not receive your payment card information. The service stores balances and transaction records to redeem purchases, handle refunds, and prevent duplicate redemption. After account deletion, de-identified transaction identifiers, product and amount records, and necessary accounting or anti-fraud information remain. Apple manages your App Store purchase history and refund requests.

Security

The app uses encrypted network connections and the system Keychain for sign-in credentials. Access to account content is restricted through authentication and service access controls. No network service can guarantee absolute security.

Website and Support Requests

These static pages do not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser details, and request time to operate and secure the website. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send to help resolve your request. Please avoid sending confidential recordings or transcripts unless necessary.

Policy Updates

This policy may change as app features, service providers, or data handling change. The updated policy and date will be published on this page.
