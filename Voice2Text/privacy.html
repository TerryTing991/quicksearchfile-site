Privacy Policy

How 繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） handles your audio, transcripts, summaries, anonymous account, and credits.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Data We Process

繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） processes audio you record or select, transcripts, summaries, custom vocabulary, processing status, anonymous account identifiers, credit balances, and Apple in-app purchase transaction data. The app also uses Apple DeviceCheck device state to prevent repeated claims of introductory credits; it does not store raw DeviceCheck tokens. The app does not collect contacts or location information and does not perform advertising tracking.

Why We Process Data

Your audio and related text are used to provide transcription, proofreading, summaries, and exports. Account and transaction information supports anonymous sign-in, job processing, credit accounting, purchase verification, refunds, and prevention of repeated purchase or introductory-credit claims. Your content is not sold or rented, or used for marketing, advertising audiences, cross-app or cross-website tracking, or purposes unrelated to these app features.

Cloud Processing and Your Consent

Transcription and AI text processing require an internet connection. Before transcription or summary generation, the app asks for your consent to cloud and AI processing. You can withdraw this consent in Settings. You can still view and export existing content after withdrawing consent, but must consent again before starting another transcription or summary. Withdrawing consent does not delete existing content or undo processing already performed.

Service Providers

Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide anonymous authentication, databases, temporary file storage, and job scheduling. Breeze running on Modal or OpenAI processes audio for speech recognition. OpenAI processes relevant transcript text and vocabulary for proofreading and requested summaries. Apple handles in-app payments and DeviceCheck checks for introductory-credit eligibility. Audio, text, and technical information are sent to the services needed for the requested feature.

Local Storage and Exports

Anonymous sign-in credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. App settings, history, and recordings used for playback may be stored on your device. When you export or share content, the destination app or service you choose handles that copy. Deleting content in 繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） does not delete copies you have exported elsewhere.

Retention and Deletion

Uploaded audio is normally removed from cloud storage after transcription completes. This cleanup is best effort; failed or interrupted uploads may remain until cleanup, individual record deletion, or account deletion. Transcripts, summaries, and local playback recordings remain until you delete the corresponding record or account.

Delete a Record or Your Account

Long-press a record in transcript history to delete its transcript, summary, and associated recording, including the server copy. To delete the anonymous account and associated content, open Settings and choose “刪除帳號與所有資料” (Delete Account and All Data). Wait for active transcription or summary jobs to finish before deletion. Account deletion also removes remaining credits and local recordings and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app alone does not request deletion of server data; Keychain credentials may also survive reinstallation.

Credits and Purchase Records

Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. 繁字逐字稿（Voice2Text） does not receive your payment card information. The service stores balances and transaction records to redeem purchases, handle refunds, and prevent duplicate redemption. After account deletion, de-identified transaction identifiers, product and amount records, and necessary accounting or anti-fraud information remain. Apple manages your App Store purchase history and refund requests.

Security

The app uses encrypted network connections and the system Keychain for sign-in credentials. Access to account content is restricted through authentication and service access controls. No network service can guarantee absolute security.

Website and Support Requests

These static pages do not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser details, and request time to operate and secure the website. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send to help resolve your request. Please avoid sending confidential recordings or transcripts unless necessary.

Policy Updates

This policy may change as app features, service providers, or data handling change. The updated policy and date will be published on this page.
