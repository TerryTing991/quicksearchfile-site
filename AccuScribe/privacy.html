AccuScribe for iOS

Privacy Policy

How AccuScribe handles the audio you choose, transcripts, summaries, your anonymous account, and credits.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Data We Process

AccuScribe processes the audio you choose or share with the app, transcripts, your transcript corrections, summaries, your selected summary sections, your vocabulary, job status, an anonymous account identifier, your credit balance, and Apple in-app purchase transaction data. The app also uses Apple DeviceCheck device state to prevent free credits for new users from being claimed more than once. A DeviceCheck token is stored on the server only temporarily while a new-user credit claim is being settled, and is deleted as soon as the claim is completed or declined. The app does not collect contacts or location and does not track you for advertising.

Why We Process Data

Your audio and related text are used only to create transcripts, proofread them, write the summaries you request, and export them. Account and transaction data are used for anonymous sign-in, job processing, credit accounting, purchase verification, refunds, and preventing duplicate redemption of purchases or new-user credits. Your content is never sold or rented, and never used for marketing, advertising audiences, cross-app or cross-website tracking, or anything unrelated to the app's features.

Cloud Processing and Your Consent

Speech recognition, proofreading, and summaries run on cloud services and require an internet connection. Before your first transcript or summary, the app explains how your data is processed and asks for your consent. If you transcribe only part of a recording because your balance can't cover the whole thing, the app trims a working copy on your device and uploads only the selected beginning. You can withdraw consent at the bottom of Settings › About › Privacy Policy. You can still view and export existing content after withdrawing, but you must agree again before starting another transcript or summary. Withdrawing consent does not delete existing content or undo processing already done.

Service Providers

Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide anonymous sign-in, the database, temporary file storage, and job scheduling. Whisper running on Modal, or OpenAI, processes audio for speech recognition. OpenAI processes transcript text and your vocabulary for proofreading and the summaries you request. Apple handles in-app payments and DeviceCheck checks for new-user credits. Audio, text, and technical information are sent only to the services needed for the feature you use, and these services may process it only to provide that feature.

Local Storage and Exports

The anonymous sign-in credential is stored in the iOS Keychain. App settings, summary-section preferences, your transcript list, corrections, and recordings kept for playback may be stored on your device. Exports are created on your device. When you export or share content, the app or service you choose handles that copy. Deleting content in AccuScribe does not delete copies you have exported elsewhere.

Retention and Deletion

Uploaded audio is normally deleted from cloud storage once the transcript is done. This cleanup is best effort; audio from failed or interrupted uploads may remain until it is cleaned up, the item is deleted, or the account is deleted. Transcripts, summaries, and recordings kept on your device for playback remain until you delete the item or your account.

Delete an Item or Your Account

Touch and hold an item on the Transcripts tab to delete its transcript, summary, and recording, including the copy on the server. To delete your anonymous account and all associated content, open Settings and choose Delete Account and All Data. Wait for active transcripts or summaries to finish first. Account deletion also removes remaining credits and recordings on the device, and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete data on the server, and the Keychain credential may survive reinstalling.

Credits and Purchase Records

Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. AccuScribe never receives your payment card information. The service stores balances and transaction records to redeem purchases, handle refunds, and prevent duplicate redemption. After account deletion, only anonymized transaction identifiers, product and amount records, and necessary accounting or anti-fraud information are kept. Apple manages your App Store purchase history and refund requests.

Security

The app uses encrypted network connections and stores its sign-in credential in the system Keychain. Access to account content is restricted by authentication and service access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Website and Support Requests

These pages do not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser details, and request time to operate and secure the website. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, to help resolve your request. Please avoid sending confidential recordings or transcripts unless necessary.

Policy Updates

This policy may change as app features, service providers, or data handling change. The updated policy and date will be published on this page.
