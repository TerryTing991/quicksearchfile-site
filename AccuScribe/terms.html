Terms of Service

Terms for using AccuScribe, including transcripts, summaries, credits, and third-party processing.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The Service

AccuScribe turns recordings you choose into English transcripts with sentence-level timestamps and can generate meeting summaries from selected transcripts. Transcripts and summaries can be exported as TXT, Markdown, Word, and PDF; transcripts can also be exported as SRT subtitles. Each recording can be up to five hours long. Supported formats include m4a, mp3, wav, aac, and caf, and you can import recordings shared from Voice Memos.

No Registration Required

You do not need to provide a name, email address, or password. After you first accept these terms and the Privacy Policy, the app creates an anonymous account to maintain credits and job status. Sign-in credentials are stored securely in the system Keychain. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. To delete it, use Settings › Delete Account and All Data.

Pay as You Go

There is no subscription or monthly fee. Credits are charged only when creating transcripts or summaries. Credits are consumable Apple in-app purchases. Apple handles purchases and purchase refund requests; spent credits cannot be restored through the app.

Transcription Credits

One credit covers one minute of a recording, billed per file. A recording shorter than one minute costs one credit. Longer recordings are billed for complete minutes only: for example, 10 minutes and 40 seconds costs 10 credits. If your balance is insufficient, you can choose to transcribe the first part of the recording.

Summary Credits

Summary credits depend on the recording’s duration: 10 credits up to 1 hour, 15 credits for 1–3 hours, 25 credits over 3 hours. The number of selected summary sections does not affect the credit cost.

Processing Failures

If transcription or summary processing fails, no credits are charged or the credits charged are automatically refunded.

Welcome Credits

Eligible new users may receive 60 credits after first accepting the terms and Privacy Policy. These credits can be used for transcripts or summaries and are limited to once per device. Reinstalling the app or creating another account does not grant additional welcome credits. The developer may change or end this offer without affecting credits already granted.

Third-Party Processing

With your permission, recordings and text are processed by OpenAI (proofreading, summaries, and fallback speech recognition), Modal (Whisper speech recognition), and Google Firebase / Google Cloud (sign-in, storage, and job processing). Your content is used only to provide the app’s features and is not used to train or improve models. See the Privacy Policy for details of the data each provider receives.

Your Responsibilities

Upload only recordings you have the right to use. When recordings involve others, obtain any consent required by applicable law. Do not use the app for unlawful content or to infringe privacy, copyright, or other rights.

Accuracy

Transcription quality depends on recording clarity, background noise, microphone distance, speaking volume, and overlapping speakers. Automatically generated transcripts and summaries may contain errors. Check important names, numbers, decisions, and action items against the original recording and decide whether the results are suitable for your use.

Storage and Deletion

Uploaded recordings are usually deleted from the cloud when transcription completes. Transcripts and summaries remain until you delete them or your account. Account deletion removes cloud content, local recordings, anonymous sign-in credentials, and remaining credits and cannot be undone.

Changes to the Service

The developer may update the service or these terms as features, laws, or providers change. Updated terms are published in the app. Continuing to use the service after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. Material changes to AI data sharing require renewed consent before processing.
