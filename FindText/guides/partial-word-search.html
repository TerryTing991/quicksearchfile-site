Guide

How to find part numbers and word fragments.

You only remember part of a code, an order number, or a word. Find Text can find it from any part.

Search from the middle of a word

Keywords of three or more letters or digits also match inside longer words. Type 4401 and Find Text finds PN-44017 in purchase orders, packing slips, spreadsheets, and notes.

Shorter keywords, with one or two letters or digits, match whole words only. That keeps short searches from matching almost everything.

Match the start of a word with *

End a keyword with * to match words that start with it. ap* finds apple and approval; contract* finds contract, contractor, and contracts.

Chinese, Japanese, and Korean

Partial-word search works with English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean text, so you can search for part of a name or term in CJK documents too.

Good uses

  • Part numbers, SKUs, and serial numbers
  • Invoice, order, and ticket numbers
  • Product codes inside longer strings, such as uv2 in ACUV2
  • Names and terms you only half remember

The same rules apply in the command line tool: ft search 4401.
