Privacy Policy

PDF Pro is a macOS viewer and non-destructive page editor for user-selected PDF documents. The app is designed around local file access and on-device processing.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Scope of Data Processing

PDF Pro processes the files and settings needed to provide its features. Depending on how you use the app, this may include selected PDF documents, document paths and file-access bookmarks, page images and metadata, embedded text, OCR text and text coordinates, search terms, page order and selection, highlights, outline boxes, arrows, text annotations, page bookmarks, bookmark notes, the last viewed page, annotation tool preferences, export and print settings, and files created with Save, Save As, Export Flatten PDF, or Export to TIFF.

The app does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or other personal information for advertising or tracking.

Purpose of File Access

PDF Pro uses macOS file panels, drag and drop, and App Sandbox permissions so you can choose the documents to open and the destinations where saved or exported files should be written. File access is used only for actions you request, such as opening a document, rendering pages and thumbnails, extracting text, searching, recognizing a selected area, printing, or writing a PDF or TIFF file.

The app does not scan unrelated folders. File-access bookmarks may be stored in the app container so previously opened documents can be restored in your workspace when macOS permits access.

Local Viewing, Search, and OCR

PDF rendering, thumbnail generation, embedded-text extraction, full-document search, and OCR run locally on your Mac. Search combines available embedded PDF text with an OCR index built on your device from the rendered page images, so search-result highlights correspond to the content displayed in the viewer. Area OCR for text, formulas, and tables also runs entirely on your Mac using models included with the app.

PDF Pro does not upload your documents, page images, extracted text, OCR results, search terms, highlights, bookmarks, or notes to a PDF Pro server for processing. The app makes no outgoing network connections of its own.

Local Storage and Temporary Files

PDF Pro may store OCR indexes, annotations, page bookmarks, bookmark notes, workspace restoration data, file-access bookmarks, the last viewed page, and app preferences in its local macOS app container or Application Support directory. Cached OCR data helps avoid repeating document-wide recognition work and may be pruned automatically.

The app may create temporary page images and working files while rendering, extracting text, editing pages, or preparing output. These temporary files are created on your Mac and are removed when the related operation finishes whenever possible.

Non-Destructive Editing and Saved Documents

Inserting, rotating, reordering, or deleting pages in the workspace does not directly modify the source document. PDF Pro writes to the original PDF only when you choose Save, and writes a new file only when you choose Save As, Export Flatten PDF, or Export to TIFF and select a destination. New files are stored in the locations you choose.

Highlights, boxes, arrows, text annotations, bookmarks, and bookmark notes are stored separately from the source PDF and are not embedded by Save or Save As. Removing a document from the current workspace does not delete the source file from disk.

Printing

When you print, PDF Pro renders the selected pages locally and passes the print job to the standard macOS printing system. Printer selection, print range, paper settings, and the handling of the print job are controlled by macOS and the printer or print service you select.

Data Sharing

PDF Pro does not sell your personal information. The app does not share your documents, OCR text, searches, annotations, or saved and exported files with third parties. Data may be disclosed only when required by law, needed to protect security, or when you explicitly save, print, copy, upload, email, or otherwise share information using another app or service.

Data Retention and Deletion

Source documents and saved or exported files remain in the locations you choose until you delete them. You can remove locally stored app data, including workspace history, file-access bookmarks, OCR indexes, annotations, bookmarks, notes, and preferences, by deleting the app and its associated container and Application Support data from your Mac.

Deleting local app data does not delete source or edited documents stored elsewhere on your Mac.

Website Data
